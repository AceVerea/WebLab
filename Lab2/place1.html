Germany

Germany is a country in Central Europe known for its rich history, strong economy, and cultural diversity. The German people value precision, innovation, and respect for tradition.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Berlin
  • Population: 83 million (approx.)
  • Language: German
  • Currency: Euro (€)

About the People

Germans are often described as polite, punctual, and hardworking. They take pride in quality craftsmanship and value efficiency in both daily life and business.

The culture highlights community, environmental awareness, and a love for festivals such as Oktoberfest.
