Sweden

Sweden is a Nordic country in Northern Europe known for its forests, lakes, and innovative design. It is also recognized for its social equality, environmental focus, and welcoming culture.

Country Overview

  • Capital: Stockholm
  • Population: 10 million (approx.)
  • Language: Swedish
  • Currency: Swedish krona (SEK)

Culture & People

Swedes value openness, work-life balance, and a strong connection to nature. The culture often emphasizes equality, innovation, and a calm, organized lifestyle.

Common traditions include enjoying fika breaks, spending time outdoors, and celebrating seasonal holidays with family and friends.
